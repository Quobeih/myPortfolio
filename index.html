About me

I stand where the classroom meets real development work. I teach programming, web, mobile, IoT and AI, and I build and ship my own systems, so the lessons I give come from projects that have actually run.

My approach is practical first. I'd rather show a working app than a slide full of theory, and I design lessons that are simple, realistic and ready for beginners.

I also use AI-assisted development and automation in my own workflow, working with coding agents to plan, build and improve systems faster.
